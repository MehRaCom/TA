Fairness methods

How each method turns remembered days into today's starting load for every person. Higher start = you carry less today (you already carried more).

Reading the numbers

  • Daily fraction = a person's load that day ÷ that day's denominator (max possible, active-that-weekday or assigned, as configured). It is a share of a day's work, so days of different size compare fairly.
  • "× task total N" is not a day count. N is the sum of today's task weights (here 5+3+1+4 = 13). Fractions are multiplied by it so starting loads are in the same units as task weights. The window length (e.g. 14 days) is separate.
  • Why 13 with a 14-day window? They are unrelated: 14 is how many days are looked at, 13 is today's total task weight.
  • Fewer days stored or first run: with 0 remembered days every start load is 0 (report says "no earlier days yet"). With 1–2 days or gaps, methods use what exists; people never present in the window get the configured new-person fill. Days older than the window or storage period are ignored.

The methods

Every day starts from zero.

Today's tasks are shared out as if yesterday never happened. Randomness alone evens things out over time.

The Classic Tally tally

Add up every remembered day.

Per day: load ÷ that day's denominator. Days are summed (or averaged). Absent days count as zero or are skipped.

Formula idea
sum or mean of daily fractions × today's task total
Best for
Long-run totals matter; easy to explain.
Watch out
Absences: choose zero (absent = got off lightly) or skip.

The Fair Shift fairshift

Average over days you were present.

Same as the tally, forced to skip absences + mean, so being away never counts for or against you.

Formula idea
mean over present days
Best for
Teams with frequent absences.
Watch out
Needs ≥1 present day or the new-person fill is used.

The Ledger ledger

Balance against the group.

Each day: your load minus the average of the people present. Positive = you carried more than your share, so you start higher. Optional credit for people who were absent.

Formula idea
Σ (my fraction − group average)
Best for
Balances relative to who was actually there.
Watch out
ledgerCredit: absent people get credited / not.

Fading Memory fading

Recent days count more.

Each day is weighted by 0.5^(age ÷ half-life) using calendar gap, so a quiet week fades naturally.

Formula idea
Σ fraction × 0.5^(age/halfLifeDays)
Best for
Fresh fairness without old debts lingering.
Watch out
halfLifeDays 1–60.

Virtual runtime.

Best for
Smooth catch-up for people who were away.
Watch out

The Season season

Fixed rounds.

History is cut into fixed seasons anchored at Saturday 2026-01-03; only the current season counts, so everyone resets together.

Formula idea
fractions inside current season
Best for
Clean fresh starts (e.g. monthly).
Watch out
seasonDays 2–60; keep window ≥ season.

The Weighted Lottery lottery

Fair odds, random pick.

Ledger balances become weights: people owed more get better odds of picking next, via softmax with a temperature.

Formula idea
ledger + weighted random pick
Best for
Fair on average yet unpredictable.
Watch out
lotteryTemperature .5–10 (higher = more random).
